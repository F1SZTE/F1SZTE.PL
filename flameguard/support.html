Can I choose between flights and trains?

Yes. Select Flights or Trains on the dashboard to see published FlameGuard services.

How do I make a reservation?

Create an account, choose an available service and seat, then confirm the reservation. It will appear under My Bookings.

How do I choose a seat?

Search for a service, select it from the results, then choose any available seat on the seat map.

Where can I get help with an RP trip?

Contact the FlameGuard team through the official channel for your roleplay community or server.

What does the fare mean?

Fares are FlameGuard roleplay points. The website does not accept real payments.
